Reinforcement Learning in Queues with Fatigue-Driven Server Failures

This project studies reinforcement-learning methods for controlling queueing systems in which server fatigue can lead to service failures.

Bayesian Semi-Parametric Joint Modeling of Longitudinal and Survival Data

In this study, we develop a flexible Bayesian method using a Dirichlet-process (DP) prior for the random-effects distribution of linear mixed models (LMMs). We then aim to develop a centering-aware MCMC algorithm for the LMM with this structurally centered, truncated DP prior.

We study explainability and reliability of neural networks from a statistical perspective, focusing on the interaction between feature attribution, uncertainty quantification and the causal validity of explanations.

A Bayesian and Machine Learning Hybrid Approach for Spatio-Temporal Data

This study investigates Bayesian spatio-temporal modelling for the estimation, mapping and prediction of relative risk across space and time. The aim is to evaluate a flexible statistical framework, based on a hybrid Bayesian and Machine Learning strategy, capable of capturing complex spatial and temporal dependencies while providing reliable predictions and uncertainty quantification.

Research Areas: Statistics , Machine Learning , Data Science

Fotini Kampa and Loukia Meligkotsidou

Development of Predictive Models for Determining Order Quantities

The project focuses on the development of predictive and analytical models for optimizing ordering decisions for high-demand products, in collaboration with a commercial company. The objective is to determine appropriate order quantities by time period, supplier, production facility, and product code, taking into account demand and sales data as well as inventory management constraints. The analysis combines forecasting models based on historical data with analytical Operations Research models and has led to the development of an automated computational application for determining the recommended order quantities.

Joint modelling of longitudinal and competing-risk data under failure cause and non-informative right censoring misclassification

In this study, we propose a unified approach to jointly model a continuous marker and competing risks in the presence of failure-cause and non-informative right censoring misclassification. In this setting, we analyze data from the East Africa IeDEA study, regarding CD4 counts.

Multi-Parameter Estimation of Prevalence: A Bayesian modeling approach to estimate the prevalence of opioid dependence

In this study, we consider a Bayesian modeling approach called Multi-Parameter Estimation of Prevalence (MPEP) to estimate the prevalence of opioid dependence. This approach leverages routinely collected administrative data, starting from a large baseline cohort of individuals from the population of interest and linked events, to estimate the size of the population of interest.

Pólya-Gamma Methods for Bayesian Learning in Contextual Multi-Armed Bandits

This project studies a contextual multi-armed bandit problem with Bernoulli rewards and latent user contexts, where learning is performed within a Bayesian framework. Each user is characterized by an unknown context value belonging to a known finite set, while the success probability of each arm depends on the user’s context through a logistic model with unknown parameters. At each decision epoch, the user’s identity is observed, but the corresponding context remains unknown. The problem involves simultaneously learning the latent user contexts, estimating the reward model, and selecting the appropriate arm or arms. Pólya–Gamma data augmentation methods are investigated for Bayesian updating and the efficient implementation of the learning algorithms.

Current

Research Areas: Operations Research , Machine Learning

Members: Apostolos Burnetas , Konstantinos Bekios

Apostolos Burnetas, Konstantinos Bekios, and Alexandros Pasiouras

Stochastic modeling of population dynamics: estimation, decision-making and optimization

This project develops stochastic models of population dynamics, with an emphasis on Markov chains, for parameter estimation, uncertainty quantification and decision optimization. It incorporates costs, rewards and control policies, exploiting the sparse structure of large state spaces to develop numerically stable and efficient algorithms. Sensitivity analysis, stochastic simulations and evaluation using real-world data support the investigation of applications in epidemiology, social dynamics and service systems.

Causal Structure Learning to Assess the Impact of Exercise Oncology on Oncological Treatment Tolerance and Clinical Course

Understanding the causal impact of exercise oncology on treatment tolerance and clinical outcomes in cancer patients remains challenging due to the complexity of longitudinal biomedical data and the presence of latent dependencies. In this study, we propose a time-series structural causal modeling framework to investigate dynamic causal relationships between exercise interventions, treatment-related variables, inflammatory processes and tumor-related biomarkers.

Bayesian Variable Selection within the Multi-Parameter Estimation of Prevalence Framework

Estimating the size of populations that are difficult to observe directly is an important problem in epidemiology and public health. The Multi-Parameter Estimation of Prevalence (MPEP) model is a Bayesian framework developed to address this problem, combining information from a known baseline cohort with linked events, such as deaths and hospital admissions. In this study, we extend the MPEP framework to incorporate Bayesian variable selection.

Current

Research Areas: Statistics , Data Science
