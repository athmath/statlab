title: "Bayesian Variable Selection within the Multi-Parameter Estimation of Prevalence Framework"
description: >-
  Estimating the size of populations that are difficult to observe directly is
  an important problem in epidemiology and public health. The Multi-Parameter
  Estimation of Prevalence (MPEP) model is a Bayesian framework developed to
  address this problem, combining information from a known baseline cohort with
  linked events, such as deaths and hospital admissions. In this study, we
  extend the MPEP framework to incorporate Bayesian variable selection.
