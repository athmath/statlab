---
title: "Bayesian Semi-Parametric Joint Modeling of Longitudinal and Survival Data"
description: >-
  In this study, we develop a flexible Bayesian method using a Dirichlet-process
  (DP) prior for the random-effects distribution of linear mixed models (LMMs).
  We then aim to develop a centering-aware MCMC algorithm for the LMM with this
  structurally centered, truncated DP prior.
team: >-
