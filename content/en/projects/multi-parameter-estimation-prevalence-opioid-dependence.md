title: "Multi-Parameter Estimation of Prevalence: A Bayesian Modeling Approach to Estimate the Prevalence of Opioid Dependence"
description: >-
  In this study, we consider a Bayesian modeling approach called
  Multi-Parameter Estimation of Prevalence (MPEP) to estimate the prevalence
  of opioid dependence. The approach uses routinely collected administrative
  data, beginning with a large baseline cohort of individuals from the
  population of interest and linking relevant events, to estimate the size of
  that population.
team: >-
  **Andreas Markoulidakis** and **Loukia Meligkotsidou** in collaboration with
  M. Hickman, M. Glancy, N. Welton and H. Jones from the School of Medicine,
  University of Bristol.
