title: "Joint Modelling of Longitudinal and Competing-Risk Data under Failure-Cause and Non-Informative Right-Censoring Misclassification"
description: >-
  In this study, we propose a unified approach to jointly model a continuous
  marker and competing risks in the presence of misclassification of failure
  cause and non-informative right censoring. We analyse CD4-count data from the
  East Africa IeDEA study.
team: >-

  New York.
