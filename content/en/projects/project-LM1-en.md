title: >-
  Joint modelling of longitudinal and competing-risk data under failure cause and non-informative right censoring  misclassification

description: >-
  In this study, we propose a unified approach to jointly model a continuous marker and competing risks in the presence of
  failure-cause and non-informative right censoring misclassification. In this setting, we analyze data from the East Africa
  IeDEA study, regarding CD4 counts.

team: >-
