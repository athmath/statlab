title: >-
 Multi-Parameter Estimation of Prevalence: A Bayesian modeling approach to  
 estimate the prevalence of opioid dependence

description: >-
 In this study, we consider a Bayesian modeling approach called
 Multi-Parameter Estimation of Prevalence (MPEP) to estimate the prevalence
 of opioid dependence. This approach leverages routinely collected
 administrative data, starting from a large baseline cohort of individuals
 from the population of interest and linked events, to estimate the size of
 the population of interest.
