title: "Development of Predictive Models for Determining Order Quantities"

description: >-
  The project focuses on the development of predictive and analytical models for optimizing ordering decisions for high-demand products, in collaboration with a commercial company. The objective is to determine appropriate order quantities by time period, supplier, production facility, and product code, taking into account demand and sales data as well as inventory management constraints. The analysis combines forecasting models based on historical data with analytical Operations Research models and has led to the development of an automated computational application for determining the recommended order quantities.
