title: "Causal Structure Learning to Assess the Impact of Exercise Oncology on Oncological Treatment Tolerance and Clinical Course"
description: >-
  Understanding the causal impact of exercise oncology on treatment tolerance
  and clinical outcomes in cancer patients remains challenging due to the
  complexity of longitudinal biomedical data and the presence of latent
  dependencies. In this study, we propose a time-series structural causal
  modeling framework to investigate dynamic causal relationships between
  exercise interventions, treatment-related variables, inflammatory processes
  and tumor-related biomarkers.
