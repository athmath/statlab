## Overview

The Machine Learning group at CENTAUR develops methods and algorithms for
learning from data, prediction, classification and decision making. Research
combines ideas from statistics, optimization and computational methods, with
applications across a broad range of scientific and technological domains.

## Research Themes

- Supervised Learning
- Unsupervised Learning
- Deep Learning
- Reinforcement Learning
- Probabilistic Graphical Models
- Computational Statistics
- Natural Language Processing
- Computer Vision
