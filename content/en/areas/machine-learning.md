## Overview {#overview}

Machine Learning is a subfield of artificial intelligence and computer science
that develops mathematical algorithms and statistical models through which
computational systems improve their performance using data and experience.
It formulates prediction, classification and generation as optimization and
statistical estimation problems rather than relying exclusively on explicitly
programmed rules.

## Research Themes {#research-themes}

Research in Machine Learning at CENTAUR covers the following learning
paradigms and specialized methodologies.

### Supervised Learning and Predictive Modelling

Supervised learning estimates relationships between input variables and
labelled outputs. Research examines the balance between model complexity and
generalization in classification and regression. Methods include parametric
and nonparametric models, support vector machines, ensemble methods such as
random forests and gradient boosting, and deep neural networks for predicting
categorical or continuous outcomes.

### Unsupervised and Representation Learning

This theme studies unlabelled data to identify latent structure, probability
distributions and informative features. Research in clustering and density
estimation includes Gaussian mixture models and hierarchical clustering for
high-dimensional data. Representation-learning methods, including
self-supervised neural networks and autoencoders, seek lower-dimensional
representations that retain relevant information.

### Deep Learning and Neural Architectures

Deep learning uses multilayer neural networks to represent complex patterns in
data. Research examines convolutional neural networks for spatial data,
recurrent neural networks and transformers for sequential data, and graph
neural networks for non-Euclidean structures. It also studies optimization and
training dynamics, including convergence, vanishing or exploding gradients,
dropout and batch normalization.

### Reinforcement Learning and Sequential Decision Making

Reinforcement learning studies how agents select actions in dynamic
environments to maximize cumulative reward. Research uses Markov decision
processes to model sequential stochastic control and develops methods such as
deep Q-networks and policy gradients for complex, high-dimensional state
spaces. Applications include robotics, autonomous systems and game theory.

### Trustworthy, Generative and Explainable AI and Applications

This theme studies the development, validation and application of advanced
artificial-intelligence models. Research in generative AI develops methods for
producing new data representations for scientific modelling. Research in
trustworthy and explainable AI develops interpretable and uncertainty-aware
explanations, examines their reliability and robustness, and addresses
security and systematic bias.

Cross-domain applications include healthcare, such as medical imaging and
genomics; finance, including risk assessment and fraud detection; and smart
infrastructure, including climate forecasting and energy-grid management.
