## Overview {#overview}

Statistics is a mathematical science concerned with collecting, analysing,
interpreting and presenting empirical data. Research in statistics develops
theory and methods for extracting information from data, quantifying
uncertainty, making predictions and supporting decisions in the presence of
randomness.

**Statistical Modelling and Inference** is central to modern statistical
science. Research in this field develops theoretical frameworks and
probabilistic models for representing real-world phenomena and drawing valid
conclusions from observed data. It also develops mathematical methods,
algorithms and estimators for inference and uncertainty quantification. Major
areas include regression analysis, generalized linear models, multivariate
data analysis and nonparametric statistics.

## Research Themes {#research-themes}

The scope of statistics ranges from general statistical methodology to
specialized applications across scientific fields. CENTAUR focuses on the
following research themes.

### Bayesian Statistics

Bayesian methods use probability to represent uncertainty and combine prior
information with observed data. Research includes computational methods such
as Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) for inference in complex multiparameter
models.

### Statistical Learning and High-Dimensional Data Analysis

This theme develops algorithms that learn from data to identify patterns,
classify observations and make predictions. Research addresses theoretical
guarantees, scalability, interpretability, uncertainty quantification and
variable selection in settings where the number of variables may far exceed
the number of observations.

### Computational Statistics and Numerical Methods

This theme lies at the intersection of statistics and computer science and
develops numerically stable and efficient algorithms for data analysis.
Research includes optimization methods such as the EM algorithm, numerical
integration, stochastic simulation and parallel computing for likelihood-free
inference and computationally intensive model estimation.

### Nonparametric and Semiparametric Methods

This theme studies statistical models that avoid restrictive finite-dimensional
parametric assumptions. Research includes the asymptotic theory of
nonparametric estimators, empirical processes and semiparametric efficiency
bounds.

### Biostatistics

Biostatistics applies statistical methods to medicine, biology and public
health. Research includes clinical trial design, longitudinal data analysis,
survival analysis, genomic data analysis and modelling the spread of infectious
diseases to support reliable scientific and clinical conclusions.

### Causal Inference

Causal inference develops methods for estimating cause-and-effect relationships
rather than describing associations alone. Research includes the design of
observational studies, counterfactual modelling, adjustment for confounding and
estimation of causal effects when randomized controlled trials are infeasible.

### Time Series and Spatial Analysis

This theme develops statistical models and methods for observations collected
sequentially over time or across geographical space. Because such observations
are often dependent, research develops models such as ARIMA models and Gaussian
processes to represent temporal and spatial correlation and forecast future
outcomes.

### Econometrics

Econometrics applies statistical methods to economic data to study empirical
economic relationships. Research addresses challenges including endogeneity,
simultaneous equations and policy evaluation through specialized regression
methods and instrumental variables.
