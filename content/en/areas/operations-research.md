## Overview

The Operations Research group at CENTAUR develops mathematical models and
computational methods for optimization, decision making and the analysis of
complex systems. Research spans both theoretical advances and applications in
industry, services, transportation, healthcare and energy.

## Research Themes

- Mathematical Programming
- Stochastic Optimization
- Dynamic Programming
- Decision Theory
- Game Theory
- Queueing Theory and Service Systems
- Supply Chain Management
- Network Optimization
