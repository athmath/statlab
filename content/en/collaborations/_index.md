## Academic Interdisciplinary & Research Collaborations {#academic-collaborations}

At CENTAUR, we believe that the most impactful scientific breakthroughs are born at the intersection of different disciplines. Specializing in the Mathematical Foundations and Applications of Statistics, Data Science, Operations Research, Machine Learning, and Applied Probability, our laboratory provides the necessary framework to support and enhance the quantitative and computational aspects of research across diverse scientific fields.
We extend an open invitation to faculty members, research groups, and laboratories within NKUA and beyond to build strong interdisciplinary bonds and jointly tackle contemporary research challenges.

Areas of Collaboration & Synergies
 - Quantitative & Computational Research Support: We contribute to the development of advanced statistical models, machine learning algorithms, and optimization methods for analyzing data and simulating complex systems (e.g., in Medicine, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Informatics, Pharmacy, and Geology).
 - Joint Research Proposals: We co-design and participate in funding proposals for national and international research programs, strengthening the interdisciplinary character of our University's submissions.
 - Co-organization of Academic Activities: We welcome the opportunity to co-organize seminars, workshops, summer schools, and joint research meetings that promote knowledge dissemination.
 - Co-supervision & Researcher Exchange: We encourage the co-supervision of master’s theses and doctoral dissertations on topics of mutual interest, offering young researchers a fertile, cross-disciplinary environment.

## Applied Research Partnerships {#applied-research-partnerships}

At the CENTAUR Laboratory, we believe that connecting advanced academic research with the needs of the economy, public administration, and society can serve as a catalyst for innovation, growth, and improved decision-making.

With expertise in Statistics, Data Science, Operations Research, Machine Learning, and Applied Probability, our laboratory provides the mathematical foundations and modern computational tools required to address complex quantitative problems across a wide range of sectors and application domains.

We extend an open invitation for collaboration to businesses, industrial and professional bodies, start-ups, public organisations, public-interest and non-profit bodies, and other organisations interested in making effective use of quantitative methods and data. These collaborations may be research-oriented or applied in nature.

How We Can Collaborate

Specialised Problem Solving: The challenges faced by businesses and organisations can be translated into appropriate mathematical and statistical models, leading to practical solutions for process optimisation, trend forecasting, and data-driven decision-making.

Joint Research Projects: CENTAUR’s scientific expertise can be combined with the practical experience, data, and needs of partner organisations to develop research synergies and participate jointly in national and European funding programmes.

Development of Tailored Models and Methods: We develop statistical models, Machine Learning methods, and Operations Research algorithms tailored to the needs of specific applications. Indicative fields include finance, healthcare, energy, transportation, and supply-chain management.

Knowledge Transfer and Scientific Consulting: We provide scientific support for evaluating, designing, and improving the quantitative methods, analytical tools, and decision-making processes used by partner organisations.
