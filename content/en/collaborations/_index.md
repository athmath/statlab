**Collaboration with Industry & the Market**

At CENTAUR, we firmly believe that bridging the gap between advanced academic research and the modern market serves as a catalyst for innovation and economic growth.
Specializing in the fields of Statistical Methods, Data Science, Operations Research, Machine Learning, and Applied Probability, our laboratory delivers the foundational mathematical expertise and cutting-edge tools required to solve complex quantitative problems.
We extend an open invitation to businesses, industrial bodies, startups, and public organizations to establish strategic partnerships, focusing on both research initiatives and—primarily—practical, real-world applications.

How We Can Cooperate
 -	Solving Specialized Problems: We transform market challenges into structured mathematical and statistical models, delivering actionable solutions for process optimization, trend forecasting, and data-driven decision-making.
 -	Joint Research Projects: CENTAUR’s expertise can be combined with practical market experience to foster research synergies and drive joint participation in nationally and European-funded programs.
 -	Developing Custom Models: Fully leveraging market data requires the development of tailored statistical models, machine learning algorithms, and operations research methods customized to the unique needs of each sector (Finance, Healthcare , Energy, Logistics, etc.).
 -	Knowledge Transfer & Consulting: We provide high-level consulting services to evaluate, refine, and upgrade the quantitative methods currently utilized by your organization or institution.

**Academic Interdisciplinary & Research Collaborations**

At CENTAUR, we believe that the most impactful scientific breakthroughs are born at the intersection of different disciplines. Specializing in the Mathematical Foundations and Applications of Statistics, Data Science, Operations Research, Machine Learning, and Applied Probability, our laboratory provides the necessary framework to support and enhance the quantitative and computational aspects of research across diverse scientific fields.
We extend an open invitation to faculty members, research groups, and laboratories within NKUA and beyond to build strong interdisciplinary bonds and jointly tackle contemporary research challenges.

Areas of Collaboration & Synergies
 - Quantitative & Computational Research Support: We contribute to the development of advanced statistical models, machine learning algorithms, and optimization methods for analyzing data and simulating complex systems (e.g., in Medicine, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Informatics, Pharmacy, and Geology).
 - Joint Research Proposals: We co-design and participate in funding proposals for national and international research programs, strengthening the interdisciplinary character of our University's submissions.
 - Co-organization of Academic Activities: We welcome the opportunity to co-organize seminars, workshops, summer schools, and joint research meetings that promote knowledge dissemination.
 - Co-supervision & Researcher Exchange: We encourage the co-supervision of master’s theses and doctoral dissertations on topics of mutual interest, offering young researchers a fertile, cross-disciplinary environment.
