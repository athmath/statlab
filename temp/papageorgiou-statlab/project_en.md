title: "Stochastic modeling of population dynamics: estimation, decision-making and optimization"

description: >-
This project develops stochastic models of population dynamics, with an
emphasis on Markov chains, for parameter estimation, uncertainty
quantification and decision optimization. It incorporates costs, rewards
and control policies, exploiting the sparse structure of large state spaces
to develop numerically stable and efficient algorithms. Sensitivity
analysis, stochastic simulations and evaluation using real-world data
support the investigation of applications in epidemiology, social dynamics and service systems.
