title: "Optimization and Learning in Service Systems"

description: >-
 The objective of the project was to develop predictive models in collaboration with a company. The goal was to determine order quantities for high-demand products from various suppliers for a specific time period per supplier / production plant / product code, taking into account parameters related to demand, sales, and inventory management constraints. The analysis was based on a combination of forecasting models using historical data and Operations Research analytical models, with the aim of developing an automated software in the form of a computer application to calculate the order quantities for each period.
